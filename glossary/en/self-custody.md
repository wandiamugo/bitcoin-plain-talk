# Self Custody

**Category:** Security

## Plain English

Keeping your Bitcoin yourself so that you, rather than a company or other person, control access to it.

## Analogy

It is like keeping your money in your own wallet instead of leaving it with someone else to hold for you.

## In Context

*""I moved my Bitcoin to a self-custody ""*

**What this means:** The person moved their Bitcoin to a wallet where they control the keys needed to access and spend it.

## Why It Matters

Self-custody gives you direct control over your Bitcoin, but it also means you are responsible for keeping your wallet and recovering information safe

- seed phrase
- Custodial wallet
