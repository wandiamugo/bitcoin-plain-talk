# Double-Spend

**Category:** Security

## Plain English

Spending the same bitcoin twice — trying to send one coin to two different people before the network catches it.

## Analogy

Like photocopying a 1,000 KSh note and paying two different shopkeepers with what look like two real notes. Only one payment can actually be honored. Bitcoin's network works like a shared ledger every shopkeeper can check instantly, so the copy gets caught before either sale is considered final.

## In Context

*""The whole point of mining is to stop double-spending.""*

**What this means:** Miners and the blockchain work together to agree on which transaction happened first, so nobody can spend the same coins in two places at once.

## Why It Matters

Physical cash can't be copied and spent twice, but digital money easily could be — this was the core unsolved problem of digital currency before Bitcoin. Understanding double-spending explains why confirmations exist, why you should wait for a transaction to confirm before treating a payment as final, and why proof-of-work matters at all.
